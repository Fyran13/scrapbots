Portal build of Scrapbots (no outside downloads).

To make the upload package:
1. Download https://cdnjs.cloudflare.com/ajax/libs/three.js/r128/three.min.js (right-click, Save as) and save it as three.min.js next to index.html in this folder.
2. Select index.html and three.min.js together and zip them (index.html must be at the top level of the zip, not inside a folder).
3. Upload the zip.
